Privacy on this website

This site introduces LUNA and links to its published Windows installers. You do not need to create an account on the website.

Your language preference

The website saves your English or Russian language choice in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to LUNA. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Downloads and external services

The site checks GitHub for the latest published installer. Downloads are served by GitHub. GitHub and the site’s hosting provider may process standard request information, such as your IP address, when providing these services. Links to external services follow their own privacy policies.

Analytics and uploads

We have not added analytics, advertising trackers or upload forms to this website. The screenshots shown here use a separate preview account; visitors’ worlds, screenshots, passwords and launcher profiles are not collected by this site.

LUNA accounts are separate

Microsoft login, LUNA friend accounts, skins and game files are handled inside the launcher and its connected services. This website does not ask for your Microsoft or LUNA password.
